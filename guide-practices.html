Best Practices Informational

  • Adapt and Modify your portfolio to the job your looking for.
  • Emphsasize your end results and details of design.
  • Visuals are preffered of text if they can sum up the result.
  • Visuals are also good since veiwers dont want to read blocks of text
  • Make sure to Spell Check before submitting your portfolios
  • Have your portfolio tell a story with each project.
  • Make it skimmable and easy to read.
  • Your first portfolio can be scrappy. Repeated feedback will polish it.
  • Make the portfolio yours, to show what best reflects you.
