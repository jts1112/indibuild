Finishing Up Tutorial

🎉 Congratulations 🎉

On completing this far into Indibuilds set of guides. We hope that you learned alot and that your portfolio journey isn't over.

Great job on getting this far through our guides. This will be a short tutorial going throught the finalizatoin process with your portfolio.

  1. Review Your Portfolio
    • Make sure to look for Spelling or Grammatical errors throught your portfolio.
    • Does your portfolio Highlight who you are and what you are capable of?
  2. Have Some One Review Your Portfolio
    • Can help find spelling and grammatical errors.
    • A person can give insight into what has worked for them in the past
  3. Prepare to Download After reviewing your portfolio you are ready to download your portfolio. If you are still within the portfolio creation page, locate the Download Button on the page. click the button to begin the download process.
