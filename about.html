About Indibuild


Our Story

What started as a fun project created by three college students, Indibuild has now become a serious upcoming company. Indibuild aims at developing a new interactive portfolio builder web application. When applying for jobs, the three founders had trouble finding an online site that could display their skills all in one place. This sparked the three founders to come together and create the idea of Indibuild. Some of the key features are that it allows users to add images, projects, introductions. In addition, we have specific settings tailored to different portfolios (swe oriented, sales oriented, business oriented etc.). We hope to help others trying to present their talents in a way they think best represents them.

Our Mission


At Indibuild our mission is to provide users with intuitive, customizable, and interactive portfolio creation. Our templates aim to enable users professionally highlight their skills in accordance with their field. Our portfolio builder will provide users with seven specifically tailored options: Software engineering, Business professional, Sales professional, Artists, Writers, Photographers, and a fully customizable template to meet their profession's needs. Goals: provide users with an intuitive portfolio creation experience that makes the process of portfolio creation easy, even for non-technical users.
